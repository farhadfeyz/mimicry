TASK:  
You will conduct a deductive content analysis on a set of units of analysis (sentences).
For each unit, assign one or more labels from a predefined coding protocol.
Your output must follow a strict structure.

1. Coding Categories & Definitions
You must code each unit using any number of the following six categories.
If a category has sub‑concepts, you must specify the exact sub‑concept.

A. Support and Promotion
Statements that:

call for supporting entrepreneurship or domestic production

praise or encourage producers, innovators, or entrepreneurs

emphasize removing obstacles, enabling, or empowering economic actors

highlight contributions to employment, development, or national progress

B. Genericizing
Statements where entrepreneurship/entrepreneurs are mentioned immediately beside other economic roles (e.g., producers, laborers, farmers, salesmen), treating them as interchangeable or part of one collective economic group.

C. Beautification
Statements that portray the country’s economic or social situation as normal, stable, or better than critics claim, often by:

framing success as accessible despite hardship

arguing that unemployment or economic problems are exaggerated

using entrepreneurship as evidence that “things are fine”

D. Ideologization
Statements linking entrepreneurship/entrepreneurs to ideological themes.
You must specify the exact sub‑concept:

Nationalism: national pride, patriotism, Iranian identity, Iranian flag, Iranian roots, Aryan race, Iran’s global progress

Islamism: Quranic references, religious teachings, Islamic values, Imams, religious duties

Anti‑Imperialism/America: explicit criticism of imperialism, America, Western domination, sanctions framed as foreign oppression

E. Solutionism
Statements that present entrepreneurship as a solution to national problems such as:

unemployment

poverty

inequality

social issues

health issues

local underdevelopment

F. Protectionism
Statements that promote domestic production and/or devalue imports, including:

praising Iran‑made goods

criticizing imported goods

emphasizing self‑sufficiency

preventing foreign currency outflow

replacing imports with domestic versions

reverse engineering foreign products

discouraging foreign branding

2. Output Format (Mandatory)
For each unit, follow this exact structure:

Code
[number]: "[unit text]" (Label1; Label2; Label3)
Explanation: One concise sentence explaining why each label applies.
Rules:

Use exact category names (Support and Promotion, Protectionism, etc.).

For Ideologizing, always specify the sub‑concept (e.g., Ideologizing – Islamism).

Multiple labels are allowed and encouraged when appropriate.

Explanations must be brief, analytical, and directly tied to the text.

3. Coding Logic (Mandatory)
Follow these decision rules:

Genericizing Rule
Apply Genericizing ONLY when “entrepreneurship” or “entrepreneurs” appear immediately beside other economic roles (producers, laborers, farmers, salesmen).
Do NOT apply it broadly.

Ideologizing – Anti‑Imperialism/America Rule
Apply this ONLY when the text explicitly:

criticizes America or imperialism

frames sanctions as foreign oppression

contrasts Iran with Western powers in an ideological way

Protectionism Rule
Apply whenever the text:

praises domestic production

criticizes imports

compares Iranian goods to foreign ones

mentions replacing imports

mentions preventing foreign currency outflow

mentions reverse engineering foreign goods

Normalization Rule
Apply when the text:

denies unemployment

claims conditions are fine

frames success as accessible despite hardship

uses entrepreneurship to argue the country is doing well

Solutionism Rule
Apply when entrepreneurship is framed as a solution to any problem.

Support and Promotion Rule
Apply when the text:

encourages

praises

calls for support

highlights contributions

asks authorities to help

frames entrepreneurship as valuable for national development
